This is where the Organization writes down how it works: what it does, who
is responsible for what, how repeated work is done, what its terms mean and
what was decided and when. People come here when they need to find something
out. Agents search here before they answer how things work in the
Organization, and they write down here what has been confirmed.

## Sections

- [About the Organization](/organization/): what the Organization does, for
  whom, how it earns its living and what guides its decisions.
- [People and roles](/people-and-roles/): who is responsible for what, who
  stands in for whom, and whom to ask about what.
- [Processes and procedures](/processes/): how repeated work is done step by
  step.
- [Procedures for agents](/agent-procedures/): how an agent does repeated
  work, what it may do on its own and where it stops.
- [Glossary](/glossary/): the Organization's terms and their other names.
- [Decisions](/decisions/): what was decided, who decided and when, and why.

## How to search

The **Search** field in the header (shortcut Ctrl+K) searches every page,
including the other names of terms. When you want to know how something is
done, start in Processes and procedures. When you do not understand a word,
look it up in the Glossary.

## How to add something

Tell your agent what you want to write down: "write down the procedure
for…", "record the decision that…" or "add the term … to the glossary". The
agent prepares the page from the section's outline and gives it to you to
review. It becomes valid once you approve it. The details are in
[How to write in the Knowledgebase](/how-to-write/).
