This section answers the questions every new colleague and every agent asks
first: what the Organization does, for whom, how it earns its living and
what it bases its decisions on.

## What belongs here

- The Organization at a glance: what it does, for whom and what sets it
  apart.
- The offer: products and services, how they are sold and to whom.
- The principles and values decisions follow.
- Where the Organization keeps what: tools, modules and who manages them.

## What does not belong here

Figures that change every month (revenue, the number of customers) and plans
in progress. This section holds what stays true for a long time. For the
current state, link to the tool where it is kept.
