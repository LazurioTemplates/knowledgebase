Example looks for ways of earning money online that are legal and proven,
adapts them under its own brand, tries them out on a small scale and expands
only what has a proven result. It is a small team of people, helped by agents
with research, overviews and drafts.

## What we do

We test every new opportunity with an [experiment](/glossary/experiment/):
with a hypothesis, a metric, a budget and the condition on which we stop it.
What an experiment confirms, we write down as a procedure and repeat. What it
does not confirm, we end and write down what we learned.

## For whom

Our first product is a research digest for small businesses: once a month we
send them proven opportunities in their field, with sources and an estimate
of the cost. We are trying it out in a pilot with a small group of readers.

## How we earn our living

From subscriptions to the research digest. We are still testing a second
sales channel.

## What guides us

1. **Evidence first.** A claim without a proven result is a hypothesis, and
   we label it as one.
2. **Only our own brand and our own data.** We do not take other people's
   texts, databases or accounts.
3. **A small test before a big one.** We launch nothing at scale before it
   has passed an experiment.
4. **Decisions are written down.** What has been decided is in
   [Decisions](/decisions/).

## Where we keep what

- Plans and their state: Mission Control.
- Procedures, terms and decisions: this Knowledgebase.
- Colours, type and writing rules: the Design System.
