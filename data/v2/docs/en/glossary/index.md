The glossary keeps the meaning of the words that matter in the Organization.
When a decision, a payment or an agent's action depends on a word, its
meaning must not live only in people's heads and in chat.

## What belongs here

- Terms the Organization uses differently from everyday language, or that
  only it uses.
- Abbreviations and the internal names of tools, teams and products.

Every term has its own page. Other names for the same thing (abbreviations,
variants in another language, slang) go in the `aliases` detail, so that
search finds them. A term never has two pages: before you add one, search for
it under its other names too.
