An experiment is a small, measurable test of one hypothesis. It has a
hypothesis, a metric, a budget, a stop condition, a
[lead](/people-and-roles/experiment-lead/) and a recorded result. It is not
launched without a stop condition
([decision 0001](/decisions/0001-no-experiment-without-a-stop-condition/)).

## Example

"Small businesses will pay for a monthly research digest in their field."
Metric: how many readers of the pilot subscribe to the digest. Budget: two
weeks of work and ten hours of an agent. Stop condition: fewer than three
subscribers after two issues.

## Not to be confused with

- **Plan**: describes work we want to do. An experiment tests whether it is
  worth it. An experiment may have its plan in Mission Control.
- **Procedure**: is written down once an experiment has confirmed something.
