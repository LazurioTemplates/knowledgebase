Use this procedure when a new colleague starts at Example. Its result is a
person who, after the first week, knows how Example plans, where the data is
and who decides what, and who has access to everything their work needs.

## Who takes part

- **Management**: prepares the access and the first task.
- **Guide**: a colleague who answers questions for the whole first week.
- **New colleague**: reads, asks and finishes a first task by the end of the
  week.

## Steps

1. **Before the first day.** Management sets up access to e-mail, the
   calendar and the team's tools, and appoints the guide.
2. **Day one.** The guide goes through this Knowledgebase with the colleague:
   the section [About the Organization](/organization/), the
   [Glossary](/glossary/) and the roles in
   [People and roles](/people-and-roles/).
3. **Days two and three.** The colleague joins a running
   [experiment](/glossary/experiment/) and follows the work of its lead.
4. **Day four.** The colleague gets a first task of their own from a plan in
   Mission Control and prepares it with their agent.
5. **Day five.** The guide and the colleague go through what was unclear.
   What was missing in the Knowledgebase, the colleague adds with their agent.

## Checklist

- [ ] The colleague has the access they need, and no more.
- [ ] The colleague knows where to find plans, procedures and decisions.
- [ ] The colleague has finished the first task.
- [ ] What was missing in the Knowledgebase has been added or written down as
  a draft.
