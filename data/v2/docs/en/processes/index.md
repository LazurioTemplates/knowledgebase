This section describes repeated work so that someone doing it for the first
time can get it right. Every procedure has an owner and the date someone last
confirmed it in practice.

## What belongs here

- Procedures that repeat: taking on an order, preparing a quote, welcoming a
  new colleague, closing the month.
- Checklists for these procedures.

A procedure that an agent carries out belongs in
[Procedures for agents](/agent-procedures/). When people and an agent share
the work, write the procedure here and the agent's part in Procedures for
agents, with a link here.
