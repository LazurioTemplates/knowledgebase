The experiment lead is responsible for one [experiment](/glossary/experiment/)
from writing down the hypothesis to the decision whether to stop it or expand
it. Every experiment has exactly one lead.

## What it decides

- How the experiment runs within the approved budget.
- Stopping the experiment when its stop condition is met.
- What agents prepare for the experiment: research, drafts of texts, overviews
  of figures.

## What needs approval

- Launching the experiment and its budget are approved by management
  (see [decision 0001](/decisions/0001-no-experiment-without-a-stop-condition/)).
- Raising the budget or extending the experiment.
- Anything Example publishes or sends outside the team: a text, an ad, an
  offer.

## Stand-in

When the experiment lead is unavailable for more than two working days,
another experiment lead appointed by management takes over the experiment.
The stand-in is recorded in the experiment's plan in Mission Control.
