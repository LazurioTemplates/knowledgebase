This section describes roles: who is responsible for what, what they decide
and whom to ask. An agent uses it to know whom to bring something to for
approval.

## What belongs here

- One page per role: what it is responsible for, what it decides without
  further approval and what needs approval, who stands in for it and who
  holds it today.
- Teams and what they focus on, when the Organization has several.

## What does not belong here

Private contacts, evaluations of people, salaries and other personal data.
Work contacts are kept in the Organization's directory; here the role and the
name are enough.
