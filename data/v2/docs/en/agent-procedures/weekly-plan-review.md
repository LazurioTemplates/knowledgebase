The agent uses this procedure every Friday morning, or when a team member
asks it for an overview of the plans. The result is a draft overview by which
the team confirms or corrects the states of the plans at the review. The agent
itself changes no state.

## What the agent works with

- Plans and roadmaps in Mission Control, read only.
- Last week's overview, if there is one.
- The roles in [People and roles](/people-and-roles/), to know who leads a
  plan.

## Steps

1. Find the plans that changed since the last overview, and the plans that
   have not changed for more than two weeks.
2. For every plan, write in one sentence what happened, and for unchanged
   plans a question to their lead.
3. Mark the plans that look blocked: waiting for a decision, for a person or
   for another plan.
4. Order the overview by the main roadmap and propose which states should
   change, with the reason.
5. Save the overview as a draft and let whoever asked for it know.

## What the agent never does on its own

- It changes no plan's state, priority or lead. The team confirms that at the
  review.
- It sends the overview to no one outside the team.
- It does not invent the reason for a delay. When it does not know it, it
  asks.

## How the agent checks the result

- Every plan in the overview links to its record in Mission Control.
- The number of plans in the overview matches the number of changed and
  unchanged plans.
- Every proposed change of state has a reason.
